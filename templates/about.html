About the project

An AI-based real-time stampede prediction and alert system for event security, transport hubs and public gatherings.

Problem statement

Crowd crushes and stampedes at pilgrimages, concerts, stadiums and stations often develop within minutes. Monitoring is usually manual: an operator watching CCTV screens notices danger only after a crowd is already visibly overcrowded, and alerting responders depends on further human steps.

Solution

{{ site.name }} continuously analyses video, measures where the crowd is densest and how fast it is growing, classifies the situation into five risk levels, and automatically notifies administrators and location-matched responders when risk reaches HIGH or CRITICAL - recording exactly who was notified and whether delivery succeeded.

Project overview

The platform has two operating modes that share one detection and risk pipeline: video analysis for uploaded footage, and live monitoring for webcam, RTSP CCTV and IP cameras. Results are stored in a database, shown on a dashboard and map, exported as PDF reports, and routed to responders through an admin-managed authority directory.
